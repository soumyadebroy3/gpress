## Known limits today

- The lossy step's photo test is cautious: an image in doubt counts as line art. Black 6 and 8 point labels on a dark part of a photograph may not be seen (#52 tried seven measures; each also caught real photographs or missed labels stored as a JPEG), so such a picture can be taken for a photo and its labels blurred at efforts 80 and 100.
- A progressive JPEG is left alone at every effort, and so is a baseline JPEG whose scan header is not as the standard writes it.
- CI builds and tests on Linux (x86-64) only.
